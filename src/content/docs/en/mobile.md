The **mobile companion** puts your agent team in your pocket: get notified
when an agent finishes, read its summary, and send follow-up instructions
without walking back to the desk.

## Getting started

1. Install the companion app — [iOS App
   Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/orca-ide/id6766130217) or grab the
   [Android APK](https://github.com/wakii-dev/wakii/releases/latest) from
   the releases page.
2. In Wakii desktop, open **Settings → Mobile** and scan the pairing QR
   code.
3. Agents, notifications, and prompts now sync to your phone.

## What you can do

- **Monitor** — live agent status, current task, and recent output
- **Get notified** — agent finished, needs input, or hit an error
- **Send follow-ups** — type a follow-up prompt; it lands in the agent's
  queue
- **Approve** — clear permission gates from the phone

## When it shines

- **Long runs** — start a big refactor on the desktop, get pinged when
  it's done, read the summary on the couch.
- **Parallel agents** — five agents in five worktrees; your phone is the
  scoreboard.
- **Quick fixes** — spot a typo in the agent's output, send the fix from
  your phone, done by the time you're back.

## Tips

- Notifications respect your phone's focus mode — agent **errors** break
  through, "done" pings wait.
- The pairing is per-machine — one QR per desktop app instance.
